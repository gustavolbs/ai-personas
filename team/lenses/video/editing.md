# Editing and post-production

Software is a tool; pacing, narrative and "every frame earns its place" separate amateurs from professionals.

## Tool choice
- **CapCut**: daily short-form, fastest captions, templates, team batch output.
- **DaVinci Resolve**: best color and audio in one free tool; long-form and ads that need grading.
- **Premiere Pro**: team pipelines, After Effects round-trips, motion-graphics templates.
- **Final Cut Pro**: fast single-editor Mac workflow, magnetic timeline.
- **Descript**: text-based editing of talking head and podcasts, filler-word removal.
- **Remotion / programmatic**: data-driven or batch variants; spec it for Dave.
Use what the project already uses. Do not switch tools mid-project.

## Pacing
Cut on action or on the beat. Remove every breath, "so", and dead frame in short-form; keep them in founder stories where authenticity is the point. A shot without new information is too long. Speed-ramp screen recordings through the boring parts, never through the moment that proves the claim. Match cut rhythm to music; land key reveals on downbeats.

## Captions as design
Always on for short-form and ads. Word-by-word or phrase-by-phrase, 1 to 2 lines, high-contrast, inside the platform safe zone, brand font when legible at phone size. Highlight the key word per phrase; never animate every word. Burn in for reels/ads; provide sidecar SRT for platforms that support it.

## Audio
Audio first, then picture. Denoise before EQ; high-pass around 80 Hz on voice; gentle compression; de-ess. Dialogue at −12 to −6 dBFS peaks, music ducked 12 to 18 dB under speech, SFX for every visual emphasis (zoom, text pop, click). Deliver at the platform loudness target (about −14 LUFS integrated for YouTube/social; ad networks specify their own). Never ship audio that clips.

## Color
Primary correction first (exposure, white balance, contrast) so every clip matches, then one look. Brand colors in graphics, not forced onto skin tones. Use LUTs as a starting point, not the grade. Check on a phone screen at 50 % brightness.

## Motion in the edit
Zooms and pans with ease-in/ease-out, 6 to 12 frames. One transition style per video; hard cuts by default. Text pops synced to the spoken word. Screen-recording emphasis: zoom to the control, dim the rest, highlight the click.

## Export
| Platform | Aspect | Resolution | fps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels / TikTok / Shorts | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | 30 | keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px |
| Feed (IG/FB/LinkedIn) | 4:5 or 1:1 | 1080×1350 / 1080×1080 | 30 | captions on |
| YouTube | 16:9 | 1920×1080 or 3840×2160 | match source | chapters, end screen 20 s |
| Paid ads | per placement | supply 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 | 30 | first 3 s carry the message; 6 s cut-down |
| Website / in-app | as designed | 1080p, poster frame | 30 | H.264 + WebM/AV1, muted autoplay variant, Dave embeds |
H.264 High profile, 10 to 16 Mbps for 1080p30, AAC 320 kbps, MP4. Check platform docs when the spec matters; they change.

## Final inspection
Watch the export on the target device, sound on and off, captions on, at the real aspect ratio. Check first frame, safe zones, spelling, claim approval, license manifest, loudness, duration. Only then hand over.
