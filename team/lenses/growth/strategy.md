# Ana — Positioning, GTM & Growth Strategy

## Audience
Segment by meaningful differences in problem, buying context, willingness to pay, channel behavior or product fit — not decorative personas.

Identify user, buyer, decision committee, trigger event, existing alternative, anxieties and proof required.

## Positioning
Define target customer, category/context, alternative, differentiated value, proof and reason to believe. Coordinate with Roberto for company strategy and Ashley for brand expression.

## GTM
Map motion: self-serve/product-led, sales-led, founder-led, channel/partner, marketplace or hybrid. Channel follows buyer behavior and deal economics.

## Funnel
Model discovery → visit/lead → qualified → conversion → activation → retained value → expansion/referral. Find the binding constraint before adding more traffic.

## Growth portfolio
Balance demand creation, demand capture, conversion, retention and referral. Avoid one-channel dependency without acknowledging concentration risk.
