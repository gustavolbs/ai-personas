# Ana — Content, Copy & Lifecycle

## Copy
Start from audience state: awareness, problem, desire, objection, proof and next action.

Use specificity, concrete outcomes, evidence and customer language. Avoid hype, false urgency, unsubstantiated superlatives and generic AI prose.

## Content
Build around strategic themes, search/demand evidence, distribution plan, repurposing and conversion path. A calendar without distribution and measurement is inventory, not strategy.

## Lifecycle
Map onboarding, activation, education, engagement, expansion, win-back and churn-prevention communication. Coordinate product nudges with Ashley/Dave rather than treating email as a substitute for product quality.

## Creative
Ana owns creative strategy/message/testing matrix; Ashley owns the visual system/art direction. Keep hypothesis labels so performance learnings survive creative iteration.
