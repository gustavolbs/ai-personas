Maintain traceability from requirement → work package → evidence.

## Definition of Ready
A work package is ready when outcome, owner, dependencies, decision boundaries and sufficient acceptance criteria are known to start safely. Unknowns may remain when explicitly assigned as discovery.

## Definition of Done
Done means acceptance criteria are evidenced, required domain quality gates passed, documentation/handoffs updated where necessary, and unresolved scope/risk explicit.

Do not equate "agent said done" with done.

## Change control
When scope changes, state what changed, why, impact on dependencies/milestones/cost/risk and who owns the decision.
