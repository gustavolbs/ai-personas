# Typography, Color, Layout & Spatial Rhythm

## Typography is interface

Choose typography for the job:
- readability at real sizes;
- x-height and character differentiation;
- numeral quality;
- weight range;
- language coverage;
- table/data behavior;
- brand character;
- rendering/performance constraints.

Define semantic roles: display, page title, section title, body, UI label, metadata, numeric/data, and technical/mono only when justified.

Operational products often need excellent numerals, compact labels and stable metrics. Marketing/read surfaces can tolerate more expressive display typography and scale contrast. Do not use marketing typography in dense work surfaces by default.

A font pair needs division of labor. One excellent family can be better than two fashionable fonts.

## Color architecture

Separate:
1. primitive palette;
2. semantic roles;
3. component/application roles.

Semantic roles include background, surface, primary/secondary text, border, action, selection, focus, success, warning, danger and info.

Color must not be the only carrier of important status.

Brand color does not need to cover the product. Operational products can feel distinctive through typography, geometry, icons and interaction with restrained color.

Dark mode is a separate surface/contrast problem, not mechanical inversion.

## Spatial rhythm

Create repeatable semantic relationships:
- icon ↔ label;
- label ↔ control;
- title ↔ support;
- row ↔ row;
- group ↔ group;
- section ↔ section.

Use a coherent scale as a tool, not a religion. Optical correction is legitimate.

## Layout grammar

Define:
- page frame;
- persistent chrome;
- content widths;
- columns;
- gutters;
- sticky regions;
- scroll ownership;
- responsive collapse.

Do not center everything by default.

## Whitespace and density

Whitespace can separate, prioritize and improve reading. It can also reduce throughput and comparison ability.

Choose density from user expertise, frequency, data volume and surface mode. Spaciousness is not synonymous with premium quality.
