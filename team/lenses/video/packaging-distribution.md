# Packaging, platform specs and iteration

Packaging decides whether the video is seen; the script decides whether it is watched. Ana owns distribution, budget and reporting; Diego owns the assets and the hypotheses.

## Thumbnail / cover / first frame
One subject, one emotion or result, maximum 3 words of text, high contrast, readable at 120 px wide. Face reacting or product outcome; avoid the platform's UI colors. The first frame of a short is its cover: design it as a still. Make 2 or 3 variants for testing.

## Titles and captions
Title states the payoff or the curiosity gap, never both vaguely. Caption copy: first line carries the hook (it is the only visible line), then context, then CTA, then hashtags (3 to 5 relevant; no hashtag walls). Include the keyword people would search for. Pin a comment with the link when the platform blocks caption links.

## Platform notes
- **Instagram Reels**: 9:16, ≤90 s for best reach; text within safe zones; trending audio only with license for business accounts.
- **TikTok**: native feel wins; fast open; on-screen text; replies to comments as follow-up videos.
- **YouTube Shorts**: loops matter; end where the start makes sense again. Long-form: chapters, 20 s end screen, strong thumbnail/title pair.
- **LinkedIn**: 1:1 or 4:5, captions on, professional context in the first line, 30 to 90 s.
- **Paid (Meta/Google/TikTok ads)**: message in 3 s, brand early, product in the first 5 s, supply every placement ratio, 6 s and 15 s cut-downs, policy check for claims. Ana supplies audience and objective; Diego supplies 3 to 5 hook variants per concept.
- **Website / app store / in-product**: muted autoplay with poster, captions burned in, ≤30 s hero, Dave embeds with lazy loading.

## Metrics per hypothesis
Each variant declares the metric it should move: hook rate (3 s views / impressions), hold rate (views at 50 % / 3 s views), completion, CTR, thumb-stop ratio, cost per result. Diego writes the hypothesis ("hook B, problem-led, should raise hold rate") and Ana reports back. Compare variants that differ in one element.

## Iteration loop
1. Ship one concept with 3 hook variants.
2. After a meaningful sample (Ana defines), keep the hook that wins on the declared metric.
3. Iterate the next weakest beat (usually the payoff or CTA), not everything.
4. Repurpose winners: cut-downs, other ratios, still versions for Ashley/Ana.
5. Record the learning in the project docs: what viewer, what hook, what result.

## Rights and compliance before publishing
License manifest complete; claims approved; disclosures for sponsorships, synthetic voices or actors where required; music cleared for the account type; captions present. Ana confirms platform policy for the ad account.
