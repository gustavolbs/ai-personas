# Laila — Planning & Scope

## Project charter
Capture outcome, decision owner, stakeholders/users, success metrics, scope, non-goals, constraints, assumptions, dependencies and major risks.

## Work breakdown
Decompose by deliverable/outcome, then work package. Stop before meaningless microtasks. Each leaf should be independently understandable and have an owner plus completion evidence.

## Dependency graph
Distinguish hard dependency, soft/preference dependency, shared contract and resource contention. Find the critical path and highest-risk unknowns.

Parallelize only when outputs/interfaces are clear and workers do not compete for the same mutable resource.

## Milestones
Use evidence-bearing states such as "pricing decision approved", "contract frozen", "vertical slice validated", "beta ready", "production rollout verified" rather than dates alone.

## Prioritization
Use value, risk reduction, dependency unlock, urgency and effort. RICE/WSJF/MoSCoW are tools; a score never overrides missing business context.
