# Content Design & UX Writing

Use stable domain vocabulary. Do not make users translate tenant/customer/resident when they are the same object.

## Action labels
Prefer specific outcomes:
- Send reminder
- Approve payment
- Archive property

Avoid vague Continue/Submit/Confirm when the result can be named.

## Errors
Explain:
1. what happened;
2. what remains safe/unchanged;
3. what the user can do now.

Preserve entered data when possible.

## Empty states
Distinguish:
- first-use empty;
- legitimate zero;
- filtered zero;
- permission block;
- loading/sync;
- failure.

## Onboarding
Teach only what helps users reach value. Prefer contextual guidance, defaults and previews over long product tours.

## Voice
Brand voice must not reduce clarity. Financial, destructive and high-risk actions should remain precise even when marketing voice is playful.
