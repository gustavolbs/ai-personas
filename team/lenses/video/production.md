# Production planning

## Storyboard and shot list
Storyboard when composition or motion is non-obvious; a shot list is enough for talking head and screen recordings. Per shot: beat reference, framing (wide/medium/close/insert), movement, subject, duration, audio, on-screen text, notes. Plan B-roll for every spoken beat longer than 4 s.

## Screen recording for software
- Record at 2× the delivery resolution (e.g. 3840×2160 for a 1080p deliverable) at 60 fps so zooms and speed ramps stay crisp.
- Clean state: demo account with realistic data, no notifications, no personal tabs, neutral theme unless the product's default is the brand.
- Cursor: large, high-contrast, click highlights; slow deliberate moves; pause on the thing that matters.
- Record one action per clip. Retakes are cheap; edits across a long take are not.
- Browser: hide bookmarks, fixed window size matching the frame, zoom level chosen so the smallest text is legible on a phone after cropping.
- Capture UI audio only when it is part of the product; otherwise mute and add SFX in the edit.
- For mobile apps: real device or simulator at exact device resolution; record touches; portrait for vertical delivery.

## Talking head
Eye line at lens height, key light 45° from the face, background with depth, lav or shotgun mic within 30 cm, 24/25/30 fps to match delivery, lock exposure and white balance. Record room tone. Speak to one person. Keep takes short; mark the good ones.

## Voice-over
Script marked with breaths and emphasis. Record 2 or 3 reads: neutral, energetic, warm. Room treated or closet. Synthetic voices need a license that covers commercial use and disclosure where the platform requires it.

## Assets and licensing
- Music: commercial license covering paid ads and the platforms used (platform-provided libraries usually exclude ads and business accounts). Keep the license file with the project.
- Stock footage/photos/fonts/icons/SFX: check commercial use, modification rights, redistribution in ads, attribution requirements.
- People: model release for anyone recognizable; consent for testimonials; do not fabricate testimonials.
- Logos and brand assets of third parties: only in permitted contexts (integration lists, comparisons with truthful claims).
- Keep a manifest: asset, source, license, scope, expiry.

## Project hygiene
Folder per video: `01-brief`, `02-script`, `03-footage`, `04-audio`, `05-graphics`, `06-project`, `07-exports`. Name clips by beat. Keep the beat sheet and the edit in sync; the beat sheet is the contract.
