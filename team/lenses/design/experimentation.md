# Design Experiments & A/B Testing

Ashley supports two different experiment types.

## Pre-build design comparison

A/B/C before production is a design decision experiment, not a statistical product A/B test.

Use it to compare architecture, hierarchy, interaction model, brand territory, visual direction, density or logo concepts.

For fair comparison keep representative content, viewport, task and fidelity similar while changing the intended dimensions.

Capture preference reasons, not only the winner.

## Production A/B testing

Once a product has traffic, define:
- hypothesis;
- eligible/exposed population;
- control;
- variant;
- primary metric;
- guardrails;
- assignment;
- duration/sample rationale;
- analysis plan.

Do not select success metrics after seeing results.

Example hypothesis:
"If the home surface becomes an exception-first queue, property managers will resolve overdue-payment interventions faster without increasing mistaken actions."

Qualitative preference and quantitative performance are different evidence. A preferred mockup can perform worse; a statistically better variant can still have usability problems.

For small founder-stage traffic, moderated comparison/usability testing may be more informative than an underpowered A/B test.
